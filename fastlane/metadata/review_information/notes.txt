REVIEW DEVICE AND BUILD
- Physical device: iPhone 17 Pro
- OS: iOS 26.6.2
- App version/build: 1.1.0 (6.1)

APP PURPOSE, AUDIENCE, AND VALUE
Vibe Habits is a private habit tracker for a general adult audience. It helps people create daily, weekly, or hourly routines, record progress, review streaks and insights, receive optional local reminders, and keep a JSON backup. Its value is a focused habit-building experience that works locally without requiring an account or sending personal habit data to a server.

ACCESS AND REVIEW STEPS
No account, sign-in, demo credentials, subscription, purchase, or sample file is required.
1. Complete or skip the three-page onboarding. Optional starter habits may be selected.
2. Open the Habits tab and tap the plus button to create a habit.
3. Use Log Progress/Edit Progress on a habit, then open Insights.
4. Open Feed to review recent activity.
5. Open Settings > Your Data to access JSON backup export and restore.
6. Small and medium Home Screen widgets show today's completed-habit count using the same private App Group container.

DATA AND DEVICE PERMISSIONS
Habit names, targets, progress logs, reminder preferences, notes, and optional photos remain on the device. Camera or photo-library access is requested only if the reviewer chooses to attach a photo. Notification permission is requested only when a reminder is enabled. The app does not request location, contacts, microphone, or App Tracking Transparency permission.

EXTERNAL SERVICES AND TOOLS
There is no external backend and no third-party service used for core functionality. The app has no advertising, analytics, tracking, external authentication, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or AI service. It uses Apple system frameworks for local storage, local notifications, photo/camera selection, widgets/App Groups, the native share sheet and Files interface for JSON backups, and the system review prompt.

REGIONAL AVAILABILITY
Functionality is consistent in every region. The only regional difference is localization: English and Brazilian Portuguese are supported.

REGULATED INDUSTRIES AND THIRD-PARTY MATERIAL
Not applicable. Vibe Habits is not intended for a regulated industry and does not provide medical, financial, legal, gambling, or other regulated services. It does not include protected third-party material requiring authorization.
