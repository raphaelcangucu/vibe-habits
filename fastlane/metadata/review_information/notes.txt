Vibe Habits does not require an account or sign-in. All habit names, targets, progress logs, reminder preferences, notes, and optional photos remain on the device. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or external account services.

To review the main functionality, finish the three-page onboarding (optional starter habits can be selected), create a habit from the Habits tab, log progress, and open Insights. A local reminder can be added while creating a habit or enabled in Settings. JSON backup export and restore are available in Settings > Your Data. Small and medium Home Screen widgets show today's completed-habit count using the same private App Group container. Camera and photo-library permissions are requested only when the reviewer chooses to attach a photo. Notification permission is requested only when a reminder is enabled.
