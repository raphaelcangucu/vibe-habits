Vibe Habits does not require an account or sign-in. All habit names, targets, progress logs, reminder preferences, notes, and optional photos remain on the device. The app does not include advertising, analytics, tracking, subscriptions, in-app purchases, or external account services.

To review the main functionality, create a habit from the Habits tab, log progress, open Insights, and optionally enable a local reminder in Settings. Camera and photo-library permissions are requested only when the reviewer chooses to attach a photo. Notification permission is requested only when a reminder is enabled.
